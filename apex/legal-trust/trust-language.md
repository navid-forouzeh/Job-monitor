# APEX — Legal & Trust Language
**Practical Professional Language for Client-Facing Documents**
*Navid Forouzeh · APEX Strategic Communication Studio · Zürich*

> This document contains practical, professional language for use across APEX's proposals, project agreements, website, and client communications. This is not lawyer-drafted legal advice — it is clear, honest professional language appropriate for a Swiss freelance studio context. For any high-value or complex engagement, consult a Swiss legal professional.
>
> **How to use this document:** Copy the relevant section into your proposal, project agreement, or website. Customize where indicated in [brackets]. Do not modify the core meaning of any clause without understanding the implications.

## 1. STANDARD CONFIDENTIALITY NOTICE

**When to use:** Include in every proposal and project agreement, in the introductory section before the scope description.

### English

> All information shared by the client with APEX — including business strategy, financial data, product details, investor materials, and internal communications — is treated as strictly confidential. APEX does not share, reproduce, or distribute client materials with any third party without explicit written permission. Client materials are used solely for the purpose of delivering the agreed project scope. APEX's use of AI tools in the production process does not involve submitting identifiable client information to third-party training datasets. A formal Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is available upon request and can be executed before the project commences.

### German

> Alle Informationen, die der Auftraggeber APEX mitteilt — einschliesslich Geschäftsstrategie, Finanzdaten, Produktdetails, Investorenmaterialien und interner Kommunikation — werden streng vertraulich behandelt. APEX gibt Kundenmaterialien ohne ausdrückliche schriftliche Genehmigung weder an Dritte weiter noch reproduziert oder verbreitet es diese. Kundenmaterialien werden ausschliesslich für die Erfüllung des vereinbarten Projektumfangs verwendet. Der Einsatz von KI-Tools im Produktionsprozess durch APEX umfasst keine Übermittlung identifizierbarer Kundeninformationen an Trainingsdatensätze von Drittanbietern. Eine formelle Geheimhaltungsvereinbarung (NDA) ist auf Anfrage erhältlich und kann vor Projektbeginn unterzeichnet werden.

## 2. DATA HANDLING STATEMENT

**When to use:** Include in proposals (in the appendix or trust section), on the website's privacy or FAQ page, and when a client asks directly how their data is handled.

### English

> **How APEX handles your data**
>
> Any documents, data, or materials you share with APEX are stored securely and accessed only by Navid Forouzeh for the purpose of delivering your project. Your materials are not shared with third parties, not used to train AI models, and not retained beyond the project lifecycle without your consent. Upon request, all client materials will be deleted from APEX's systems within 14 days of project completion. APEX uses cloud-based tools (including AI writing and research tools) during the production process; however, client-identifying information is not submitted as part of AI prompts where this can be avoided, and any AI tools used are configured to disable data retention where that option is available. While APEX takes data protection seriously and operates in alignment with reasonable privacy expectations, this statement does not constitute a formal GDPR compliance declaration. For engagements requiring formal data processing agreements, please request one before the project commences.

### German

> **Wie APEX mit Ihren Daten umgeht**
>
> Alle Dokumente, Daten oder Materialien, die Sie APEX zur Verfügung stellen, werden sicher gespeichert und ausschliesslich von Navid Forouzeh für die Durchführung Ihres Projekts verwendet. Ihre Materialien werden nicht an Dritte weitergegeben, nicht zum Training von KI-Modellen genutzt und ohne Ihre Zustimmung nicht über den Projektlebenszyklus hinaus aufbewahrt. Auf Anfrage werden alle Kundenmaterialien innerhalb von 14 Tagen nach Projektabschluss aus den Systemen von APEX gelöscht. APEX nutzt cloudbasierte Tools (einschliesslich KI-gestützter Schreib- und Recherchetools) im Produktionsprozess. Identifizierende Kundeninformationen werden jedoch, soweit möglich, nicht als Teil von KI-Prompts übermittelt, und verwendete KI-Tools werden, sofern verfügbar, so konfiguriert, dass die Datenspeicherung deaktiviert ist. APEX nimmt den Datenschutz ernst und handelt im Einklang mit angemessenen Datenschutzerwartungen. Diese Erklärung stellt jedoch keine formelle DSGVO-Konformitätserklärung dar. Für Aufträge, die einen formellen Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag erfordern, bitten wir Sie, diesen vor Projektbeginn anzufordern.

## 4. STANDARD PAYMENT TERMS

**When to use:** Include in every proposal and project agreement, in the pricing section. Present these terms clearly — do not bury them in small text.

> **Payment Terms — APEX**
>
> **Deposit:** 50% of the total project fee is due upon project confirmation. Work does not commence until the deposit is received. Confirmation is defined as: the client's written acceptance of this proposal (email confirmation is sufficient).
>
> **Final Payment:** The remaining 50% is due before delivery of the final project files. Final files (slides, documents, PDFs) are released only after the final payment is confirmed. This protects both parties and ensures a clean handover.
>
> **Payment Method:** Bank transfer (IBAN). Invoice details will be provided upon project confirmation.
>
> **Currency:** All fees are quoted and invoiced in Swiss Francs (CHF).
>
> **Payment Period:** Invoices are due within 10 business days of the invoice date.
>
> **Late Payment:** Invoices not settled within 30 days of the due date are subject to a late payment fee of 5% per month on the outstanding amount. APEX reserves the right to pause project work on any other active engagements with the same client until overdue invoices are settled.

## 5. REVISION POLICY LANGUAGE

**When to use:** In the scope section of every proposal, immediately after the deliverables list. Be specific about how many rounds are included for each offer tier.

> **Revision Policy**
>
> APEX includes [**2 rounds**] of consolidated feedback within each [SIGNAL / COMMAND] project package. A revision round is defined as: a single set of feedback submitted by the client in one message or document, covering all changes requested at that stage of the project. APEX will implement all changes within the agreed scope.
>
> Revisions are not unlimited iterations — they are a structured part of the process. To get the most from each round, clients are encouraged to consolidate all feedback before submitting.
>
> **What constitutes a scope change:** Any request that materially alters the strategic direction of the project, adds new sections or deliverables not included in the original brief, or requires rebuilding work that was already approved in a prior review round — this falls outside the included revision scope.
>
> **Additional revisions** beyond the included rounds are billed at **CHF 150 per hour**, rounded to the nearest 30 minutes. APEX will always flag when a request approaches or exceeds scope before proceeding, so there are no billing surprises.

*Note for Navid: For the CLARITY package (starter tier), include 1 revision round. For SIGNAL, 2 rounds. For COMMAND, 2 rounds plus a final QC review before delivery.*

## 6. PROJECT SCOPE STATEMENT

**When to use:** In the scope section of every proposal, after the deliverables list and before the timeline. This sets expectations upfront and prevents the most common source of client friction.

> **Scope & Project Boundaries**
>
> This proposal defines the scope of work for the project described above. The deliverables listed represent the full scope of APEX's engagement under this agreement. Any work beyond this scope — including additional deliverables, significant changes to the strategic direction after the architecture phase is approved, or requests that require rebuilding previously completed sections — will be discussed and agreed upon separately before work proceeds.
>
> **How scope growth is handled:** If the project evolves in a way that would require meaningful additional work, APEX will notify the client in writing before proceeding. A brief scope addendum will outline what additional work is involved and what it will cost. No additional work is billed without the client's explicit approval.
>
> APEX is committed to delivering the agreed project efficiently and on time. Clear scope boundaries protect the client from unexpected costs and protect the quality of the work — scope creep is the most common reason projects run late and over budget. If you have questions about what is or isn't included, ask before the project starts.

## 7. AI USAGE DISCLOSURE

**When to use:** Include in every proposal (in the methodology or process section), on the APEX website FAQ, and whenever a client asks about your process. Be proactive about this — it builds trust rather than eroding it.

> **APEX's Use of AI Tools**
>
> APEX uses AI tools — including large language models — as part of the research, drafting, and quality-review process. This is a standard part of modern strategic communication work, and APEX is transparent about it.
>
> What this means in practice: AI is used to process research, generate initial narrative drafts, explore structural options, and stress-test logic. Every piece of AI output is reviewed, curated, edited, and refined by Navid Forouzeh before it becomes part of your deliverable. The strategic thinking, editorial judgment, narrative architecture, and final positioning are APEX's intellectual work — AI is a tool in that process, not a replacement for it.
>
> What this does not mean: Your materials are not fed wholesale into public AI systems. Client-identifying information is handled with discretion. The final product reflects APEX's judgment and expertise, not an unreviewed AI output.
>
> If you have specific requirements around AI tool usage for your engagement — for example, if your organization prohibits the use of certain tools — please raise this before the project commences and APEX will accommodate where possible.

## 8. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITY STATEMENT

**When to use:** In every proposal, in the process or timeline section. Frame it positively — as a partnership expectation, not a legal disclaimer.

> **Client Responsibilities**
>
> For APEX to deliver the best possible outcome, the client takes responsibility for the following:
>
> **Accuracy of information:** All information provided in the intake questionnaire, briefing documents, and client communications is the client's responsibility to verify. APEX builds strategic communication on the foundation of what the client shares. If key information changes after the project has commenced, please notify APEX immediately.
>
> **Content approval:** Before final files are released, the client is responsible for reviewing all content for factual accuracy, including financial figures, team information, market data, and any claims made in the materials. APEX will flag areas that require client verification, but final approval rests with the client.
>
> **Timely feedback:** APEX works to agreed timelines. Late feedback from the client is the most common cause of missed deadlines. If feedback cannot be provided within the agreed review window (typically 48 hours for minor rounds, 72 hours for full deck reviews), please communicate this in advance so the project timeline can be adjusted accordingly.
>
> **Point of contact:** The client will designate a single primary point of contact for the project. Feedback from multiple stakeholders should be consolidated before submission to APEX.

## 9. NO GUARANTEED OUTCOME DISCLAIMER

**When to use:** Include in every proposal — in the final section, before the signature/confirmation block. Keep it brief and professional. Do not over-apologize for it — state it matter-of-factly.

> **A Note on Outcomes**
>
> APEX's work is strategic communication — we build the clearest, most compelling version of your story. A well-crafted pitch deck, board presentation, or executive narrative materially improves your ability to communicate effectively with investors, boards, and decision-makers.
>
> However, APEX cannot guarantee fundraising success, investor interest, term sheet outcomes, or any specific business result. Investment decisions are made by investors, not by pitch decks. The deliverable is a high-quality communication tool built on your strategy — it creates the conditions for a strong conversation. What happens in that conversation depends on many factors beyond the deck.
>
> APEX is committed to delivering work that represents the strongest possible version of your pitch. The goal is a deliverable you are confident walking into any room with.

## APPENDIX — DOCUMENT ASSEMBLY GUIDE

**For a standard proposal, include sections in this order:**

1. Project overview (scope, deliverables, timeline)
2. Revision Policy (Section 5)
3. Project Scope Statement (Section 6)
4. Payment Terms (Section 4)
5. Standard Confidentiality Notice (Section 1)
6. AI Usage Disclosure (Section 7)
7. Client Responsibility Statement (Section 8)
8. No Guaranteed Outcome Disclaimer (Section 9)
9. Data Handling Statement (Section 2) — optional, include if client is particularly data-sensitive or in a regulated industry

**For the APEX website, include:**
- Fictional Showcase Disclaimer (Section 3) — on portfolio page
- AI Usage Disclosure (Section 7) — on About or FAQ page
- Data Handling Statement (Section 2) — on Privacy or FAQ page

**When signing an NDA before sharing materials:**
Execute the NDA first, then proceed with the intake questionnaire. Reference the NDA in the project agreement. The Confidentiality Notice (Section 1) still applies within the project agreement even if an NDA is also in place.
