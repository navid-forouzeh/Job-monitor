### Day 1 — Positioning + Brand Decision
**Time estimate: 2–3 hours**

**Primary Task:** Lock in your brand positioning. By end of day, you must have decided: who you serve, what you do, how you're different, and what you're called.

**Secondary Tasks:**
- Register domain (apexcommunication.ch or alternative via Hostpoint/Infomaniak)
- Create business email (contact@yourdomain.ch via Zoho Mail free tier)

**Daily Tasks:**
1. Write your one-sentence positioning: *"I help [specific target] [specific outcome] through [specific mechanism]."* Spend 30 minutes on this — max.
2. Confirm brand name: APEX is the working name. Only change it if you have a concrete, better alternative. Decide in under 20 minutes.
3. Define your 3 target client segments (DACH founders, executives, investors — be specific about company stage and size)
4. Write the "what I do" paragraph — 4–6 sentences for LinkedIn About section
5. Pick 2 brand colors (recommend: near-black + a single accent — gold, amber, or cobalt)
6. Pick 2 fonts (recommend: one serif for headlines, one clean sans-serif for body — available on Google Fonts)
7. Register domain
8. Set up business email

**What "Done" Looks Like:**
- A Notion page or Google Doc with: positioning statement, target segments, "what I do" paragraph, brand name confirmed, 2 colors noted with hex codes, 2 fonts noted
- Domain registered and confirmed in inbox
- Business email active and sending/receiving

## MONTH 1 HONEST ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

*Answer these five questions on Day 30. Answer them honestly, not optimistically. This is your decision tool.*

---

### Question 1: Did the message land?

**What to measure:** Out of all the outreach messages you sent, what was your response rate?

- Above 15%: your message is resonating — the problem is volume or conversion
- 5–15%: your message is mediocre — test a different angle or different target
- Below 5%: your message is not landing — stop everything and rewrite it

**Decide:** Is the issue the message, the target, or both?

---

### Question 2: Did the calls convert?

**What to measure:** Of the calls you had, how many moved to a proposal conversation?

- Above 50%: your offer and positioning work — the problem is getting more calls
- 25–50%: your discovery process needs work — you're not uncovering pain clearly enough
- Below 25%: your offer may not match what the market actually needs

**Decide:** Do you need more calls (a pipeline problem) or better calls (a conversion problem)?

---

### Question 3: Did the content build credibility?

**What to measure:** Are the right people engaging with your LinkedIn posts? Are you being reached out to (inbound), or only reaching out (outbound)?

- At least one inbound inquiry from content: content is working — increase frequency
- Only outbound generating conversations: content is building awareness but not converting — improve the call-to-action and specificity
- Zero engagement from target audience: content is not reaching the right people — change topics, hashtags, or targeting

**Decide:** Is LinkedIn a credibility tool (working) or a distraction (not yet working)?

---

### Question 4: Is the offer right?

**What to measure:** When you explain your offer, do people understand it immediately and express interest, or do they look confused or skeptical?

- Immediate understanding + interest: the offer is clear — keep it
- Understanding but no interest: the offer is clear but not compelling — the problem you're solving may not be urgent enough
- Confusion: the offer is unclear — simplify the language and narrow the scope

**Decide:** Does the offer need to change, or just the way you explain it?

---

### Question 5: Is this the right market?

**What to measure:** Who actually responded to your outreach? Are they the clients you expected, or a different profile?

- If the most engaged people were the target you planned: stay the course
- If a different segment responded more (e.g., executives rather than founders, or VCs rather than startups): consider pivoting your primary target to where the energy is
- If nobody responded: the DACH market may require more trust-building before they engage with an unknown studio — consider building credibility through content and referrals before cold outreach

**Decide based on these five answers:**
- If 3+ answers are positive: **accelerate** — increase volume, raise prices, systematize
- If 2–3 answers are mixed: **persist and adjust** — the model is directionally right, tune the execution
- If 4–5 answers are negative: **pivot** — not necessarily the business, but the target, offer, or channel needs a meaningful change before Month 2
