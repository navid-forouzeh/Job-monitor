## DAY 2 — OFFERS + TEMPLATES

*End of Day 2 output: ready to send a proposal the moment someone says yes*

- [ ] Write what they receive: a 30-minute discovery call, a written narrative brief (2–3 pages), and a 6–8 slide structural outline with commentary
- [ ] Write the timeline: 3–5 business days from kickoff call
- [ ] Set the price: CHF 900 (non-negotiable; this is a quick, high-leverage engagement)

**SIGNAL Deck (Offer 2)**
- [ ] Write offer name and tagline (example: *"SIGNAL Deck — A pitch deck that earns the meeting"*)
- [ ] Write who it's for: seed-to-Series B founders preparing for investor fundraising rounds
- [ ] Write what they receive: a 45-minute discovery call, complete deck architecture (narrative + slide structure), full visual production (10–14 slides), one revision round, final delivery in PDF and editable format
- [ ] Write the timeline: 10–14 business days from kickoff call
- [ ] Set the price: CHF 3,200 (this is your flagship offer; do not discount it)

**COMMAND Retainer (Offer 3)**
- [ ] Write offer name and tagline (example: *"COMMAND Retainer — Strategic communication, on demand"*)
- [ ] Write who it's for: executives and founders who regularly need high-quality presentations, board decks, or external communications
- [ ] Write what they receive: up to 2 presentation projects per month, a 30-minute monthly strategy call, priority turnaround (5 business days per project), access to revision rounds
- [ ] Write the timeline: ongoing monthly engagement, minimum 2-month commitment
- [ ] Set the price: CHF 2,500/month

**Payment Terms + Operations**

- [ ] Create client onboarding questionnaire using Notion (a simple table or page form) or Typeform (free plan). Include: company name, client name and role, project objective, current communication challenge, target audience, key messages, deadline, and any existing materials.

## DAY 5 — OUTREACH SYSTEM

*End of Day 5 output: 30 prospects identified, first 10 messages drafted and ready to send*

**Prospect Research**
- [ ] Open LinkedIn and search for your 3 target segments. Use these search strings:
  - Founders: *"Founder OR CEO" + "Series A OR Seed" + "Switzerland OR Germany OR Austria"*
  - Executives: *"CFO OR CMO OR CPO" + "Zürich OR Munich OR Vienna"*
  - Investors: *"Principal OR Partner" + "VC OR Venture" + "DACH OR Switzerland"*
- [ ] Identify 10 founder prospects: note Name, Company, LinkedIn URL, Company Stage, one specific detail (recent post, funding news, product launch)
- [ ] Identify 10 executive prospects: same fields
- [ ] Identify 10 investor/ecosystem contacts: same fields
- [ ] Save all 30 in a Google Sheet or Notion database with columns: Name | Company | Role | LinkedIn URL | Connection Degree | Specific Detail | Message Status | Response | Notes

**Outreach Template**
- [ ] Write your outreach message template. It must follow this structure:
  - Line 1: One specific observation about them or their work (reference the detail from your research)
  - Line 2: What APEX does and who it's for (one sentence)
  - Line 3: A low-commitment ask
  - Example: *"Your post on building a Series A narrative caught my attention — the point about investor pattern-matching is exactly what I work on. I run APEX, a Zürich-based communication studio that helps DACH founders and executives structure their most important stories. Would a 20-minute conversation be useful?"*
- [ ] Write 10 personalized versions — one for each of your first 10 prospects. Each message must reference the specific detail in your database.
- [ ] Save all 10 messages in your database (paste into the Notes column, or create a "Message Draft" column)

**Follow-Up Sequence**
- [ ] Write a follow-up message template for use 5–7 days after the initial outreach. Keep it to 2–3 sentences. Do not apologize. Add one new piece of value. Example: *"Following up on my previous message. I published a piece on LinkedIn this week about [relevant topic] that might be relevant to what you're building. Still happy to connect if the timing works."*
- [ ] Save follow-up template in your Notion or Google Doc outreach system

**Calendly**
- [ ] Confirm Calendly is connected to your business email — test by booking a slot yourself
- [ ] Enable email reminders (24 hours before and 1 hour before) in Calendly settings

**LinkedIn Post Draft**
- [ ] Write a first draft of LinkedIn Post #1. Don't publish today — publish on Day 7. Topic: introduce APEX, your background, who you serve, and what you're building. Hook first sentence. No self-congratulation. Focus on the problem you're solving for the people you want to reach.
