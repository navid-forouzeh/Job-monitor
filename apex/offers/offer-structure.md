# OFFER 1 — "CLARITY" Strategic Narrative Sprint

## Offer Name Card

**CLARITY** is APEX's standalone written narrative engagement. Before you build a deck, you need to know what it says — and why each word is there. CLARITY produces the 2–3 page strategic document that becomes the foundation for every investor conversation, pitch deck, and executive summary you send for the next 12 months. It is the clearest version of your story, written at the standard your investors actually read at.

*For founders who need to test and sharpen their story before committing to a full deck. For executives who need a crisp external brief. For anyone who has been told their pitch "needs work" and received no further guidance.*

---

## What It Is

A standalone written strategic narrative document — 2 to 3 pages. Not slides. Not a template filled in. A purpose-built document that articulates the investment thesis, the problem, the solution, the market, the traction, and the ask in a single coherent argument that an investor, partner, or board member can read in under 8 minutes and understand completely.

This is the document founders use before the deck exists, alongside the deck as an executive leave-behind, or independently as a first engagement filter for investor conversations. It is the single most useful document a fundraising founder can have.

---

## Price

**CHF 950 — fixed price, no hourly billing.**

*The pricing psychology:* CHF 950 is the price of one hour of McKinsey partner time, one mediocre designer revision, or approximately 0.2% of a CHF 500K raise. It is priced to be a genuinely easy decision for a founder with a real business — low enough to remove hesitation, high enough to signal that this is serious professional work. In the Swiss market, anything below CHF 700 for a strategic document reads as a commodity. CHF 950 signals premium craft without requiring a procurement approval.

---

## Timeline

5 business days from receipt of the completed client brief and approval of scope. The clock starts when APEX has everything needed — not when the project is signed.

---

## Ideal Client

A founder who has a working business or a clear thesis but cannot yet articulate it in a form that passes institutional scrutiny. They may have a deck in draft form that is not working, or no deck at all. They want to validate and sharpen the narrative before spending CHF 3,000+ on a full deck. They have an investor conversation, an accelerator application, or a partnership discussion on the horizon within 30–60 days.

---

## Exact Deliverables

1. **Strategic Narrative Document** — 2–3 pages, written in English, structured as: Problem, Insight, Solution, Market, Why Now, Why Us, Traction (if applicable), Ask
2. **Message Hierarchy Map** — a single-page outline showing which claims are primary (must land), secondary (should land), and tertiary (support only) — for use in all subsequent communication
3. **3 Opening Lines** — three alternative framings for the first sentence of any pitch or deck, each with a different rhetorical approach (data-led, narrative-led, provocation-led)
4. **Revision brief** — a written annotation of what was changed from client input and why, so the client understands the strategic reasoning behind every structural decision

---

## Required Client Input

APEX requires all of the following before work begins. Incomplete briefs delay the 5-day clock.

1. **Completed APEX Brief Form** — provided upon engagement; covers company description, stage, round size, investor target type, current traction, team background, and any existing materials
2. **Existing materials** — any current deck, one-pager, website link, or investor email the client has already used (even if it is not working — especially if it is not working)
3. **One 30-minute onboarding call** — scheduled within 48 hours of project start; this is mandatory, not optional
4. **The ask** — a clear statement of what the client is raising, from whom, and by when; if this is unknown, CLARITY is premature

---

## Promised Outcome

After CLARITY, the client will have a written articulation of their investment thesis that is structurally sound, audience-calibrated, and ready to inform every subsequent pitch asset. They will not leave with a perfect deck — they will leave with the thinking that makes any deck possible. Founders who complete CLARITY and proceed to SIGNAL typically require 20–30% fewer revision rounds because the narrative foundation is already established.

*This is not a guarantee of investor interest or a funded round. Strategic clarity is a necessary condition for a strong pitch. It is not a sufficient one.*

---

## What Is Excluded

- Slide design or deck production of any kind
- Financial model review or valuation input
- Market research or primary data collection
- More than one narrative document (e.g., separate versions for different investor types)
- Translations into German, French, or any language other than English
- Revision of the Message Hierarchy Map or Opening Lines after delivery (these are fixed deliverables)

---

## Revision Policy

- **1 round of structured revisions** is included — the client submits consolidated written feedback within 5 business days of delivery; APEX responds within 3 business days
- Revisions must address strategic or factual issues in the narrative — not stylistic preference changes
- A second revision round is available at CHF 250, invoiced separately
- Feedback submitted after the 5-day revision window forfeits the included revision round

# OFFER 2 — "SIGNAL" Pitch Deck Architecture

## Offer Name Card

**SIGNAL** is APEX's core offer: a full pitch deck built from strategy through to final slides, delivered in PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides. SIGNAL is not a design project — it is a communication architecture project where design is the final output, not the starting point. The result is a 10–15 slide deck that your investors will actually read, built on a narrative structure that holds up under questioning.

*For founders actively raising Pre-Seed through Series A. For executives preparing board presentations or partner decks where the stakes are real.*

---

## What It Is

A complete pitch deck — strategy, narrative architecture, slide structure, written content, and visual direction — produced across 10–15 slides and delivered in the client's preferred format. APEX owns the strategy and narrative; the client receives a deck that is both visually credible and structurally sound.

SIGNAL begins with strategic diagnosis (what does this business actually need to say, and to whom), proceeds through narrative architecture (what is the argument, in what order, with what evidence), and ends with slide content and visual direction. No slide is built before its strategic purpose is defined.

---

## Price Tiers

**Tier 1 — CHF 2,500**
For founders with a completed CLARITY narrative document, a clear brief, organised existing materials, and a straightforward business model. The narrative foundation is established; SIGNAL translates it into slides. Recommended for Pre-Seed decks with a defined investor audience and round size under CHF 1M.

**Tier 2 — CHF 3,500**
For founders starting without a CLARITY document, or with a more complex business model requiring deeper strategic input before slide structure can be established. Includes an additional strategy session and a more extensive narrative architecture phase. Recommended for Seed rounds, decks targeting multiple investor types, or situations where the current deck exists but is structurally broken.

**Tier 3 — CHF 4,500**
For Series A decks, board presentations for established companies, or any engagement requiring secondary research integration, multiple audience versions, or a narrative that must hold up under formal due diligence scrutiny. Includes full strategic diagnosis, narrative architecture, content production, and one optional secondary deliverable (one-pager, executive brief, or leave-behind document).

*Tier selection is confirmed during the initial diagnostic call, not unilaterally by APEX. If a client qualifies for Tier 1, they are told so.*

---

## Timeline

10–14 business days from receipt of complete client materials and first strategy session. Tier 1 engagements typically land at 10 days. Tier 3 engagements typically land at 14 days. Timeline extensions beyond 14 days are communicated at the midpoint check-in, not at delivery.

---

## Ideal Client

A founder actively in or approaching a fundraising process at Pre-Seed through Series A who understands that their current deck is not performing at the level their business deserves — or who is building their first deck and wants to do it correctly rather than iterating from a broken starting point. Also: executives at growth-stage companies who need a board presentation or partner deck that reflects the sophistication of the proposition being made.

---

## Exact Deliverables

1. **Strategic Diagnosis Memo** — 1 page, written before slide work begins; documents the thesis, audience profile, key claims, and structural approach APEX will use; client approval required before production starts
2. **Narrative Architecture Map** — a visual outline of the deck's argument: slide titles, primary claim per slide, supporting evidence type, and sequence logic; delivered before slide content is written
3. **Full Slide Content** — written copy for all slides: headlines, body content, data callouts, and speaker notes for slides that require them; written in the client's voice, calibrated to the target investor type
4. **Visual Direction Brief** — a written and annotated guide to the visual style, colour approach, layout logic, and data visualisation choices used in the deck; can be handed to any designer for execution if the client has their own design resource
5. **Designed Deck** — 10–15 slides, fully designed and formatted in PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides (client specifies at brief stage); APEX design is clean, professional, and institutional — not decorative
6. **Investor FAQ Preparation** — 5–8 anticipated investor questions with recommended response frameworks; written, not verbal; delivered with the final deck
7. **Delivery call** — 30-minute walkthrough of the final deck, including rationale for key structural decisions

*Tier 3 only: one additional deliverable — one-pager, executive brief, or leave-behind — produced to match the deck narrative*

---

## Required Client Input

1. **Completed APEX Signal Brief** — provided upon engagement; 2–3 pages covering business description, round details, investor target profile, current traction metrics, team, competitive landscape, and any previous investor feedback received
2. **All existing materials** — current deck (if one exists), financial model summary, product screenshots or demo link, any press or third-party validation
3. **Strategy session** — 60-minute call within the first 2 business days; mandatory
4. **Midpoint review** — client must review and respond to the Narrative Architecture Map within 2 business days of delivery, or the production timeline extends accordingly
5. **Metric accuracy confirmation** — client is responsible for the accuracy of all data, statistics, and financial figures included in the deck; APEX is not liable for errors in client-supplied figures

---

## Promised Outcome

A structurally rigorous, visually credible pitch deck that clearly communicates the investment thesis, demonstrates market understanding, and anticipates the questions a serious investor will ask. Founders who use SIGNAL decks enter investor meetings having already answered the first seven questions in writing — which changes the quality of the conversation.

*This is not a guarantee of investor meetings or a funded round. SIGNAL produces the strongest version of the communication asset. What happens in the room is determined by the business, the market, and the founder.*

---

## What Is Excluded

- Financial modelling or valuation analysis
- Investor list building or outreach support
- Video production, animation, or interactive deck formats
- Translation into any language other than English
- Versions tailored to more than one investor audience (additional versions available at CHF 750 per version)
- Content for appendix slides beyond what is produced within the 15-slide limit
- Post-delivery redesign for a different software format than specified at brief stage

---

## Revision Policy

- **2 full rounds of structured revisions** are included — one after the Narrative Architecture Map, one after the first complete deck delivery
- Each revision round requires consolidated written feedback from the client submitted within 5 business days of delivery
- A revision round is defined as: one set of consolidated feedback addressed in one APEX response — not a rolling back-and-forth
- Changes to business fundamentals (pivot in business model, change in round size or structure) after the Strategy Diagnosis Memo is approved are treated as a scope change and invoiced at CHF 350/hour
- A third revision round is available at CHF 400, invoiced separately

---

## Pricing Justification

If you are raising CHF 500,000, you are asking an investor to make a decision about your business based substantially on how it is communicated. SIGNAL at CHF 2,500–4,500 represents 0.5%–0.9% of a CHF 500K raise. A single additional investor meeting obtained because the deck was structurally compelling — one meeting that did not happen with the previous version — is worth more than the entire SIGNAL fee. The question is not whether SIGNAL is expensive. The question is whether a weak deck is cheap.

A traditional design agency engagement of equivalent scope runs CHF 8,000–15,000 in Zürich and produces a deck that looks professional but may lack strategic depth. A McKinsey engagement addressing pitch narrative and structure is inaccessible at under CHF 40,000. SIGNAL is the only option at this price tier that begins with strategy and ends with a designed deck.

# OFFER 3 — "COMMAND" Monthly Strategic Communication Retainer

## Offer Name Card

**COMMAND** is APEX's ongoing monthly partnership for founders and executives who do not have a one-time communication problem — they have a continuous one. Investor updates, board decks, partner presentations, executive narratives, due diligence materials, and internal strategic briefs: the output of building a serious company never stops. COMMAND means APEX is in your corner every month, with guaranteed capacity, priority response, and the institutional communication standard applied to everything that goes out.

*For scale-up founders, heads of communications, and CEOs who need a strategic communication partner they do not have to re-brief from scratch every engagement.*

---

## What It Is

A 3-month minimum monthly retainer providing ongoing strategic communication support. Each month includes two major strategic communication deliverables, unlimited smaller revisions to existing APEX-produced materials, one 60-minute strategy call, and priority response across all communication channels. APEX functions as the client's embedded strategic communication resource — available, calibrated, and context-carrying.

---

## Price Tiers

**Tier 1 — CHF 2,800/month**
For clients requiring 2 major deliverables per month with standard complexity: investor updates, one-pagers, short executive briefs, revised deck sections. Suited for founders who have a stable narrative and need consistent monthly output without strategic reset. Includes 1 strategy call per month.

**Tier 2 — CHF 3,500/month**
For clients requiring 2 major deliverables per month with moderate-to-high complexity: board presentations, multi-section strategic documents, new investor narrative versions, or partner decks requiring original strategic thinking. Includes 1 strategy call per month and unlimited smaller revisions on all APEX-produced materials in the client's library.

**Tier 3 — CHF 4,200/month**
For clients with high monthly output requirements, multiple stakeholder audiences, or ongoing strategic narrative evolution (e.g., a company in active fundraising, post-term-sheet, or preparing for a Series B). Includes 2 strategy calls per month, 2 major deliverables with no complexity restriction, unlimited smaller revisions, and a monthly narrative audit — a written review of what is working, what has shifted, and what needs to be updated in the client's core communication assets.

*A "major deliverable" is defined as: a new deck of 8+ slides, a new written narrative of 1,500+ words, a new executive brief, or a board presentation. Minor revisions, one-pager updates, email framing advice, and content edits to existing materials do not count against the major deliverable allocation.*

---

Monthly ongoing with a 3-month minimum commitment. Deliverables are produced on a rolling schedule agreed at the start of each month. APEX commits to a maximum 48-hour first response to any new request and a maximum 5-business-day turnaround on major deliverables.

## CROSS-OFFER NOTES

### The Natural Client Journey

The architecture of the three offers is designed to reflect how client relationships actually develop:

1. **CLARITY** removes the first barrier — it is low-risk, fast, and immediately useful. Many clients who would not commit CHF 3,500 to a deck will commit CHF 950 to a narrative document. After CLARITY, the relationship is established and the upgrade conversation is natural.

2. **SIGNAL** is the core commercial engine. Most clients will enter at SIGNAL directly (if they have a clear mandate and budget) or graduate from CLARITY. The CHF 250 CLARITY credit is the bridge.

3. **COMMAND** converts the best SIGNAL clients into ongoing relationships. The retainer conversation should happen at the SIGNAL delivery call, not weeks later — identify during delivery which clients are going to have ongoing needs and name it.

### Referral Policy

Clients who refer a paying client to APEX receive a CHF 250 credit on their next invoice (for retainer clients) or on their next project (for one-off clients). Referral credits do not expire within 12 months of issuance. This should be communicated proactively at the close of every engagement.

### Discount Policy

APEX does not discount on price. APEX may adjust scope on a fixed-price engagement if a client's requirements genuinely fall below the standard scope — in which case the Tier 1 price applies. "I cannot afford CHF 3,500 for SIGNAL" is a scope conversation, not a discount conversation: the answer is either Tier 1 eligibility or a deferred project start.
